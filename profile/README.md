## Our focus

- **Software engineering:** enterprise applications, APIs, and system integration.
- **Applied AI:** AI agents, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and document intelligence.
- **Data and automation:** data pipelines, analytics, and traceable business workflows.
- **Reliable operation:** evaluation, access controls, observability, and human oversight.

## Engineering principles

Start with a clear business problem. Define scope and acceptance criteria. Build incrementally, validate with representative evidence, and keep security and human review part of the design.
